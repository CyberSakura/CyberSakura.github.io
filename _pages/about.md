## About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Tulane University](https://sse.tulane.edu/cs), advised by [Prof. Rebecca Faust](https://rjfaust.github.io/). I work at the intersection of human-computer interaction, visualization, and software engineering, combining qualitative studies, interactive prototyping, and program analysis to help people understand and improve software and data workflows.

Previously, I completed an M.S. in Software Engineering at UC Irvine with [Prof. Joshua Garcia](https://jgarcia.ics.uci.edu/), and a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University.

For research themes and ongoing projects, see [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).
