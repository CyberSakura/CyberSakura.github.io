CodeMentor explores LLM-integrated tutoring for programming education. The project combined interviews and surveys, thematic analysis, personas and task scenarios, heuristic evaluation, and usability testing, culminating in a Node.js prototype with lesson navigation, adaptive chat tutoring, and progress tracking.

**Timeline:** Sep 2024 – Dec 2024
